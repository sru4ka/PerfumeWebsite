Good questions

Frequently asked

Is price comparison really free?

Yes. Comparing prices across retailers is always free. The premium tier adds price intelligence — history charts, drop alerts, and the full Deals experience — plus unlimited shelf, wishlist, scans and Ask.

Where do the fragrance notes come from?

Note pyramids are compiled from public brand descriptions and general knowledge, and flagged as such. Perfumedia uses zero data scraped from community fragrance databases.

How does the daily pick work?

It's deterministic — the same inputs always produce the same pick — and drawn only from fragrances you own. It factors in the day's weather and your Scent DNA, and shows its reasoning so you can trust it.

Which platforms are supported?

Perfumedia is built with React Native + Expo. It's iOS-first, runs on Android, and there's a web build too. Your collection stays in sync.

Do you sell my data or take a cut when I buy?

Buying uses affiliate links, always disclosed under FTC guidelines — it never changes your price. Comparison stays free and your collection is yours.
