What nobody else does

Four things that exist in exactly one fragrance app

A pick that reads the weather

One recommendation each morning, drawn only from bottles you own, tuned to the day's conditions and your scent profile — and it shows its reasoning instead of asking for trust.

Real price intelligence

Retail struck through against live listings, per-millilitre value so sizes compare honestly, drop alerts on your wishlist, and suspicious listings filtered before you ever see them.

An identity that drives the picks

Twelve Perfume Personalities, from The Sovereign to The Alchemist. Free to take and built to share — and your result quietly tunes every recommendation the app makes, so the matches get sharper the more it knows.

Reviews with something at stake

Reading is free; posting takes a membership. Every review, rating and vote comes from someone who paid to be here — and thin data is labelled early rather than dressed up as consensus.

Parfumo tells you about a perfume. Perfumedia tells you what to wear today, and where to buy your next bottle for less.
