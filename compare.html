The honest read

Where the others are genuinely ahead

Parfumo owns depth and time

231,000 fragrances against our 50,000, plus roughly two decades of accumulated community reviews. That gap is real and it will not close by launch. It is also why the argument is never “our database is better” — it is that a bigger encyclopedia does not tell you what to wear on Thursday or stop you overpaying by ninety dollars.

Fragram proved the price point

A scan-and-recommend app with a hard paywall at $7.99 to $49.99, sitting at 4.7 stars across 1.8K US ratings. It validates that this audience pays for AI utility — and it has no collection, no community, and no price comparison to defend. That is the shape of the opening.

Free is the market's default

Parfumo, Perfumist and Fragrantica are all free, so the free tier has to be genuinely good or the paid tier never gets a hearing. That is exactly why browsing, live deals and the quiz sit outside the paywall: they are the argument, not the product.

Parfumo tells you about a perfume. Perfumedia tells you what to wear today, and where to buy your next bottle for less.
